
A logarithm is the inverse functions of exponential functions.

$$
b^m = x
$$
Logarithms are basically: 

- By what power $m$ must I raise $b$ by, to achieve result $x$.

$$
\log_b(x) = m
$$
**Examples:**

Exponential Function:
$$ 
2^5 = 32
$$
Logarithmic Function:
$$ \log_2(32) = 5 $$

### Natural Logarithms

Natural logarithms are the inverse function of the exponential function $e^x$, where $e$ $e \approx 2.71828$
$$
\ln(x) = \log_e(x)
$$
### Algebraic Functions

Product Rule:
$$
\log(x) + \log(y) = log(xy)
$$
Quotient Rule:
$$
\log(x) - \log(y) = \log(\frac xy)
$$
Power Rule:
$$
\log(x^k) = k\log(x)
$$
### Change of Base Formula:

To change the base of a logarithm from one base $B$ to base $b$:
$$
\log_B(x) = \frac{log_b(x)}{\log_b(B)}
$$
